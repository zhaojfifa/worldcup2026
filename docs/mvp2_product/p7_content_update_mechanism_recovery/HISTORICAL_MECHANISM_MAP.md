## One-paragraph summary
Nothing in the slate/share/ambassador/projection stack is deleted, hidden, or overwritten — it is all on main and wired, and the daily SLATE update backbone is fully connected. The genuine losses are: (1) the **content-production pipeline** (data/model frame → LLM narrative) is **fixture-locked and never invoked by the daily loop**, so daily hotspots are hand-authored; (2) `MatchDesk.tsx` is dead code; (3) `selected_hotspot_<date>.json` is disconnected; (4) there is **no internal update-mechanism page** (only `/internal/growth`); (5) the manual-fixture **next-day recap carryover** is wired only for numeric-id fixtures; (6) the strong-call assembly is **duplicated** in `mvp2_growth_cli.py` and `strongCallProjection.ts` (drift risk, plus a stale "Brazil vs Morocco" hardcode in the CLI recap branch).
